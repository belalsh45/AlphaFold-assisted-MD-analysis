# Exploratory interpretation of MD outputs

## Structural deviation (RMSD)

The 50 ns plots show an initial rise in protein Cα RMSD, followed by fluctuations in the approximately 2–4 Å region. Protein-aligned ligand heavy-atom RMSD also fluctuates. These measures describe deviations under their respective alignment and reference definitions; **they do not on their own prove ligand retention, equilibration, or stable binding**.

## Global compactness (radius of gyration)

The displayed radius-of-gyration trace varies relatively modestly around approximately 29–30 Å, with fluctuations and some slow changes over time. This is compatible with limited global expansion/contraction during the reported trajectory, but is not proof of convergence.

## Residue-level flexibility (RMSF)

Most annotated regions have lower Cα RMSF than several flexible segments; a few peaks appear markedly elevated. Interpretation of individual residues requires mapping trajectory-topology indices to the actual sequence and ensuring the RMSF alignment/measurement is specified.

## Protein–ligand contacts

The plotted occupancy values show several contact residues recurring in a high fraction of analyzed frames. High occupancy is conditional on the contact definition, analyzed timeframe, and sampling and **cannot be directly interpreted as binding energy or affinity**. Some residue labels and indices may be identifying; screen before public upload if required by the laboratory.
